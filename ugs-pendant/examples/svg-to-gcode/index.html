SVG to G-code Preview · 0.3.3

Plot, cut or trace vector paths. Finish every pass on a path before moving on.

Presets

Saved in UGS across sessions. Includes import, tool, origin and ordering settings; excludes artwork and individual path edits. Use an existing name to replace it.

1 · Artwork

Physical SVG dimensions take priority. DPI affects pixel-based sizes. Smaller spacing gives finer curves and larger files. Text/clones must be converted to paths in Inkscape.

Merges nearby redundant samples before scaling. Endpoints and bends changing the outline by more than 0.001 mm are retained. Reimport to apply; the SVG file is unchanged.

Custom coordinates are in the scaled, rotated SVG coordinate system (Y upward). This changes output coordinates, not machine zero.

2 · Tool and passes

Laser passes repeat at focus Z. Match M3/M4 and the S range to your controller. Power is disabled for travel and lowering.

Use Surface Z, Final Z and Step down above for progressive passes, just as in plotter mode. Example: 0 → −1.2 with step 0.5 cuts at −0.5, −1.0, −1.2. On a floating Z axis these Z changes set pressure, not measured material depth.

Uncheck to turn corners at the current cutting Z. XY swivel arcs remain. Entry alignment is controlled separately below.

Swivel Z must be at or above the first cutting Z and still keep blade contact. Safe travel Z must fully release and clear the blade.

Leave repeats at 1 for one cut at each Z. Turns at or above the threshold use swivel feed and, if enabled, lift to swivel Z. Smaller turns stay at cutting Z.

Uncheck to remove all entry turns and lead-ins. Corner swivels stay enabled. With alignment on, lead-in 0 removes only the straight lead-in; the entry turn remains when direction changes.

Orange lines show the HOLDER center moving, not the blade-tip cut. In an ideal swivel, the holder turns around the tip to face the next cut. Contact turns can still mark material. Positive lead-ins move the tip too; their position is not checked for scrap or neighboring shapes. Knife output uses M5 to turn the spindle off and never turns it on.

1 pass per path
3 · Paths

Click near the selected path to lock a start. Open paths use an endpoint. Manual starts and direction choices survive order changes; reimport clears them. Reversing a closed path keeps its start. For open paths, picking an endpoint sets direction; changing direction clears that endpoint lock.
